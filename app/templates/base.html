About this service

JeevanRekha is a structured triage and parallel emergency-routing service for maternal and newborn danger signs. Fixed questions, deterministic rules, two routing tracks, honest live status, and automatic escalation until a human confirms help is moving.

JeevanRekha is a decision-support and emergency-routing service. It is not a diagnostic tool, not a doctor, and not a source of medical advice. Triage follows fixed, publicly-documented danger-sign categories from government community-health training material; every unclear answer escalates by design. In any emergency, follow the instructions of health workers and local authorities.
